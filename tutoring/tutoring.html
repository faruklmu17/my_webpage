Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the tutoring experience.

What age group do you teach?

My tutoring sessions are optimized for students aged 9 to 18 (Grades 4-12). I adapt the curriculum complexity and pacing based on the student's age and experience level.

How do virtual tutoring sessions work?

Sessions are conducted live via Zoom. We use screen sharing, interactive coding environments (like Replit or VS Code Live Share), and digital whiteboards to make the experience highly collaborative and engaging.

Do I need any prior coding experience?

Not at all! I specialize in teaching complete beginners as well as advancing intermediate students. We start exactly where you are and build from there.
