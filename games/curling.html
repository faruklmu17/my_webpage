Pro Curling 2026

How to Play

  • Aim & Shoot: Drag anywhere on screen to set power and direction.
  • Set Curl: Use the slider to rotate the handle left or right before shooting.
  • Sweep: Hold SPACE while the stone is moving.

Sweeping Mechanics 101

Sweeping warms the ice, creating a thin layer of water. This does two things:

  • Reduces Friction: The stone glides further.
  • Reduces Curl: The stone travels straighter.

Pro Tips

"If you see your stone curling too much or slowing down too early, SWEEP! But be careful—if you're already going too fast, sweeping will make it worse!"

Game Info & Tips

About the Game

Pro Curling 2026 is a strategy sports game where two teams slide stones on a sheet of ice towards a target area. The goal is to get your stones closer to the center (the "button") than the opponent's stones.

Winning Strategy

  • The Hammer: The team that shoots last (has the "hammer") has a huge advantage. They can knock out opponent stones with the final shot.
  • Guards: Place stones in front of the house to protect your scoring stones.
  • Takeouts: Use high power to knock opponent stones out of play.

Advanced Sweeping Tips

  • Extend Distance: If a shot is light (too slow), sweep immediately and keep sweeping!
  • Hold the Line: If a stone is curling too much towards a guard, sweep to make it run straighter.
  • Don't Sweep if the stone is too heavy (fast) or tracking incorrectly—let the ice slow it down or curl it away.
