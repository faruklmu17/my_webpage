🎯 Your Scraping Missions

Mission 1 – Basic Extract

Scrape every hero-name and hero-alias. Print them as a list.

Mission 2 – Group by Category

Group heroes by their data-column attribute and print each group.

Mission 3 – Power Ranking

Extract power level numbers and sort heroes from strongest to weakest.

Mission 4 – Top Abilities

Collect all ability-tag values and find the 5 most common abilities.

Mission 5 – Export CSV

Save id, name, alias, team, universe, power level, and rank to a .csv file.

Mission 6 – Find Weaknesses

Scrape the weakness-row for every hero and list them neatly.

Mission 7 – Universe Filter

Separate Marvel vs DC heroes using the meta-tag universe text.

Mission 8 – JSON Export

Build a JSON with each column as a key and its heroes as an array of objects.
