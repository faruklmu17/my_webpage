Support: ChatGPT Stats Overlay

Need help or want to report an issue? This page covers common questions and troubleshooting steps.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Reload chatgpt.com after installing or updating the extension.
  • Open chrome://extensions and confirm the extension is enabled.
  • If the overlay disappears, try a hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac).
  • If you updated files, click Reload on the extension card in chrome://extensions.

FAQ

Q: Does this extension read my chat messages?
A: No. It tracks activity signals (tab focus + interaction) to calculate active time, and stores your notes locally.

Q: Why does it ask for “User activity” in Chrome disclosures?
A: Active time requires detecting interaction (mouse/keyboard) to avoid counting idle time. The extension does not log specific keystrokes or content.

Q: Is my data uploaded anywhere?
A: No. Data stays on your device in Chrome local storage.

Q: Why isn’t active time increasing?
A: Active time only counts when:

  • the tab is visible and focused, and
  • you interacted recently (mouse/keyboard activity).

Q: What is the optional import?
A: If you enable import and manually select an exported conversations.json file, the extension can compute extra summary stats locally. Import is optional.

Q: How do I reset everything?
A: Uninstall the extension or clear extension storage from Chrome. Uninstalling removes all stored local data.
